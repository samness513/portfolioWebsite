Featured work

Aerospace Engineering 3D Modeling and Structural Simulation Project Management

CySat CubeSat Prototype

I am the project manager for CySat, Iowa State University's Cube-Satellite program. I have been involved in every phase of the design process of a CubeSat prototype, from initial design through launch.

I joined CySat in the fall of 2025 as a systems and integration team member. I designed parts of the structure in SolidWorks and fabricated them using 3D printers. I also soldered several of the PCBs inside the satellite and created camera mounts.

In the spring of 2026 I became the Operations Team Lead. In that role I designed and soldered PCBs for use within the prototype, created the team's Standard Operating Procedure, and led 5 other students.

This past summer I traveled to the NASA Columbia Scientific Balloon Facility in Texas to integrate our CubeSat prototype for launch on a high-altitude balloon. After the successful prototype test flight, we started work on CySat-2, planned for launch in 2028. As project manager this semester, I have presented CySat to a NASA delegation including Administrator Isaacman and created the requirements for CySat-2.
